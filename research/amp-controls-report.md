# What each amp control does, measured

Generated by `research/analyse_amps.py` from 103 amps measured on the Quad Cortex (CorOS 4.1.1). Run it again after the sweep to refresh this file.

**One phrase, one cab, one take.** Every number comes from a 3.5 s stretch of `clip-20261118-135138-b5ac1a` replayed through each amp into `412 CA Stand OS S V30 90s (M)` at its default microphones. On the typical amp the loudest band below 200 Hz is 27.4 dB under the loudest band overall, and from 8 kHz up it is 52.8 dB under. The bass and low-end controls of 99 amps were therefore measured again on the stretch of a take with the most energy below 200 Hz, and their figures come from that pass. Bands more than 45 dB under an amp's loudest band are left out. Another guitar, phrase or cab would give other numbers. Nothing here has been listened to.

Knob effects are dB per point as the knob rises, where a point is a tenth of the knob's travel. "Below" is measured from the default down, "above" from the default up. Band changes are relative to the rest of the spectrum. Level is given separately.

# Tone Lab codebase review

November 20, 2026. Reviewed the default `lab/` rewrite at `ceb9a6e`, its Windows launcher, and its interfaces with the older `tone_lab/` app. Compared with `main` at `1f1a4c3`. The rewrite has a useful split between the desk, the Quad Cortex worker, and audio analysis. I would keep it out of `main` until Stop, preset protection, and playback routing are reliable.

## Fix before merging

1. **Stop can leave the device playing, and Undo can restart it.** With a hardware command in flight, Stop sets a flag that also discards the queued command meant to disconnect audio. In the simulation the desk showed a stopped state while the engine kept playing. The same flag can discard Save or Record when they are queued immediately after Stop. Independently, turning a knob during Guitar playback, stopping, and pressing Undo restored Guitar mode and queued playback again. Give Stop a guaranteed way to disconnect audio and keep playback state out of edit undo. [Stop and undo](../lab/desk/studio.py#L135) · [Worker command loop](../lab/qc/host.py#L44)
2. **Opening the desk can discard unsaved edits on the Quad Cortex.** The rewrite records the selected slot and scene, then can recall its own slot while restoring the desk. It does not check that the original preset is clean. A simulated unsaved gain edit reverted from 0.88 to the saved 0.50 after opening and closing the engine. Protect the original preset before any recall and treat unknown edit status as a reason to wait. [Engine open and show](../lab/qc/engine.py#L76) · [Existing borrowed-slot guard](../lab/qc/slots.py#L195)
3. **A failed volume write can leave Guitar connected at full preset level.** In a simulated unit that ignored the requested lane trim, the engine connected the input at 0 dB instead of −35 dB. Readback then raised an error, with the route still connected and the engine unset as playing. Verify mute and trim before connecting the input. On failure, disconnect any route added by the operation. [Apply and verify a chain](../lab/qc/unit.py#L201) · [Engine quiet](../lab/qc/engine.py#L187)
4. **Paired playback can omit half of a two-row sound.** A simulated preset with a serial connection from row 1 to row 2 was eligible for paired playback. When I selected it on B, the engine sent the first row directly to Multi Out and left the second row disconnected. The measurement path uses the full routing, so the desk can describe a sound the player is not hearing. Preserve the complete route for each side, or use per-preset recall for routing the paired path cannot represent. [Paired playback](../lab/qc/engine.py#L145) · [Measurement routing](../lab/hearing/listen.py#L47)
5. **A preset switch on the unit can leave the Phrase loop feeding the new preset.** I reproduced a native recall to a USB-fed preset with the old DI still streaming. A later scene selection also acted on that preset. Verify ownership while streaming and before scene changes, and stop sending audio when the selected slot changes. [Loop and scene changes](../lab/qc/engine.py#L160)
6. **Save can write a different sound under the name you confirmed.** With the inline name entry open for B, I selected A and confirmed. The simulated device saved A under B’s name. Bind the confirmation to the sound ID, scene, and knob settings it was opened for. [Inline save confirmation](../lab/desk/window.py#L738) · [Saving a sound](../lab/desk/studio.py#L941)
7. **An incomplete directory response can make Save overwrite an existing preset.** The slot lookup treats a missing entry the same as an explicitly empty slot. In the simulator, omitting slot 0 from the directory response caused Save to overwrite the preset actually stored there. Require a complete indexed directory and confirm the destination is vacant immediately before saving. [Slot lookup and allocation](../lab/qc/slots.py#L73) · [Saving to the unit](../lab/qc/engine.py#L293)
8. **The desk can silently lose completed jobs.** It tests every future twice when moving finished work out of the queue. If a future completes between the two passes, it is removed without its result callback running. I reproduced this with a future that changed state between checks. Process each future once per tick so completion always updates the desk. [Job completion](../lab/desk/studio.py#L152)

## Desk workflow and stored sounds

- **Copy B to A shares knob settings.** The two sides reference one stored sound ID. Turning Drive on B also changes the stored Drive value on A. Undo restores the old IDs while leaving the overwritten knob value in place. An undone tone selection also returned after reopening because Undo skipped saving the restored state. Give A an independent snapshot and persist the restored tone and knob values after Undo. [Copy B to A](../lab/desk/studio.py#L831) · [Undo](../lab/desk/studio.py#L135)
- **Changing the phrase can keep playing the previous audio.** Choosing a new take updates the desk’s selection. Even asking the engine to play the new audio leaves its existing loop buffer in use. Replace the loop when the take or trim changes and preserve the cursor only while the input is unchanged. [Phrase selection](../lab/desk/window.py#L774) · [Loop creation](../lab/qc/engine.py#L160)
- **Recording lost the paired wet take and can present an incomplete capture as ready.** The default desk saves dry only. It displays “play now” before the hardware worker has opened the stream. Simulated ASIO overflows were ignored and an incomplete 0.03-second take was saved and selected. Record dry and stereo wet together, signal readiness from the recorder, and check the return before it becomes the active phrase. [Desk recording](../lab/desk/studio.py#L963) · [Hardware recording](../lab/qc/engine.py#L321) · [Audio callback](../lab/hearing/audio.py#L116)
- **Blind comparison can reveal the preset and hides the preference controls.** In the hidden Tk test, starting Guitar displayed the preset name on the status line. Keep and Not this were unavailable while Blind was active. Leave those controls available under A and B and reveal names after the choice. [Blind rendering](../lab/desk/window.py#L226) · [Playback status](../lab/desk/studio.py#L255)
- **A failed worker startup can leave the desk waiting indefinitely.** When simulated ASIO initialization failed, the worker reported the error through its readiness future and exited. The later desk-open command stayed pending. Propagate the startup error to the desk and fail subsequent commands until a worker is ready. [Worker startup and replies](../lab/qc/host.py#L106)

## Measurement and comparison

- **A/B can present measurements from the wrong take or settings.** While a measurement was pending, I selected another take and changed Drive. The old measurement was stored under the new take’s name and the saved Drive change disappeared. Changing the loop bounds also keeps the same phrase ID. Loudness matching uses prior measurements without checking the current phrase, scene, or knob settings. If one side is replaced by an unmeasured tone, the previous trim can stay on the other. Capture the phrase bounds, scene, chain digest, and knob settings at dispatch. Use that identity to decide whether evidence applies, and merge measurements into the latest sound record. [Background measurement](../lab/desk/studio.py#L897) · [Loudness matching](../lab/desk/studio.py#L266)
- **Invalid return audio can teach the system a preference.** The audition verifies sample counts and accepts full-length silence or clipping. Synthetic silence produced a complete set of features that the taste model accepted. Reject silent, clipped, and invalid returns before saving any measurements. The harmonic probe also folded synthetic opposite-phase stereo to near zero, labeling the same nonlinear signal clean where its in-phase form measured crunch. Measure energy per channel before combining it. [Return checks](../lab/hearing/listen.py#L47) · [Harmonic probe](../lab/hearing/probe.py#L143) · [Taste features](../lab/player/taste.py#L237)
- **One preference mark can be counted as several separate choices.** In an offline test, one keeper mark among eight previously played candidates produced eight reported choices and a strong preference. Count independent decisions and keep the actual A/B audition context with each one. [Preference evidence](../lab/player/taste.py#L199)

## Assistant and listening history

- **The daily limit can be exceeded, and Cancel can leave an earlier request active.** With 79 logged calls, a foreground and background call both started and produced 81. The quota is only checked before calls and recorded after they finish. A second request also replaces the first request’s cancel handle. I reproduced the older answer installing a tone after Cancel was pressed on the newer request. Reserve a quota slot when starting and use request IDs to govern cancellation, result application, and shutdown. [Assistant ledger](../lab/sound/assistant.py#L68) · [Request ownership](../lab/desk/studio.py#L302)
- **The privacy description is broader than the actual data flow.** The README says everything about the player stays on the computer. A captured test prompt for a named-song background round contained a synthetic Spotify-derived artist summary. Distinguish local raw exports and recordings from the summaries sent to the assistant. Make use of listening history explicit and omit it from prompts that do not need it. [Privacy description](../README.md#personal-data) · [Listening context](../lab/desk/studio.py#L625) · [Assistant prompt](../lab/sound/design.py#L524)
- **Combining standard and extended Spotify history can abort analysis.** A local-format date and a UTC date were each valid on their own. Together, the summary raised a date comparison error. Normalize timestamps before comparing them and cover a mixed export in the import tests. [Listening history dates](../lab/player/listening.py#L72)

## Test results

- All **771 automated tests** passed. The suite emitted a Tk callback warning, and the Git whitespace check flagged three trailing blank lines.
- Targeted tests used the real desk widgets in a hidden Windows window, the simulated Quad Cortex and worker, scripted assistant replies, and synthetic audio. They reproduced the workflow failures described above outside the existing suite.
- Tests used simulated hardware and assistant responses. Listening quality, hardware timing, and the behaviour of the physical unit during recovery remain to be verified on a live rig after the playback and ownership fixes.

## Status after the fixes (November 20, 2026)

Every finding above was reproduced on the simulated unit and fixed; `tests/test_lab_review_faults.py` holds one test per fault, written to fail on the code as reviewed.

1. **Stop.** A stop now covers only the commands sent before it: the running one ends at its next safe point and the waiting ones are dropped, but anything sent afterwards (the disconnect, Save, Record) runs. Undo puts back the sounds and their knob positions and never changes what is playing; it is saved for the next start.
2. **Unsaved edits.** The engine refuses to open, and refuses any recall or build, while the player's preset has unsaved edits or the unit does not say (as the old app did). The desk says so and tries again every five seconds.
3. **Levels before inputs.** `Unit.apply` reads back every row's level and mute before connecting any input, and takes the input off any row it was connecting when the change fails.
4. **Paired playback.** Each side is played with its own routing over its two rows, so a row feeding the next stays joined.
5. **Moved presets.** While something plays the desk asks the unit every 1.5 s whether its slot is still the app's; if the player chose another preset, the phrase stops at once and that preset is left alone. Scene changes check the same.
6. **Save.** The inline name is bound to the sound and knob positions it was opened for.
7. **Directory.** A slot missing from the listing is an incomplete listing, never an empty slot; the destination is checked again just before writing.
8. **Jobs.** Each finished job is looked at once per tick.

Desk workflow: Copy B to A makes a sound of its own. A new take or new loop edges replace the loop that is playing. Recording keeps the dry take and the selected sound's stereo return, says "play now" only when the driver delivers audio, raises on dropped audio or a short take, and keeps nothing silent or clipped. Keep and Not this stay available while comparing blind; a choice shows the names, and playing blind no longer names the sound. A worker that cannot start fails every command at once.

Measurement: a measurement carries the phrase and the part of it it was made on and the chain digest, is merged into the latest record of the sound, and is used for loudness matching only when it holds for what plays now (with the knobs, only the shaped measurement for those knobs); otherwise both sides play at the preview level. Silent and clipped returns raise instead of being measured. The probe measures the louder channel instead of averaging. A keeper among several designs is one decision whose pairs share its weight; the model counts and resamples decisions, and A/B choices keep their phrase, scene and knob positions.

Assistant and listening history: a call takes its place in the daily allowance when it starts. Cancel drops every request in flight, each with its own signal. The README says exactly what goes to the assistant; background rounds no longer include the listening history. Standard and extended Spotify dates are both read as UTC.

Still to verify on the live rig: Stop and the ownership check during real playback, the level read-back on the real unit, and recording through ASIO.
